**Stack CMS** is a content management platform for editing and personalising 3D stacks. Its standout feature is the ability to spin up personalised links that track detailed analytics — perfect for embeds, proposals and anywhere you need to know how a prospect engaged.

## What I worked on

- **Editing experience** — a React interface for composing and personalising 3D stacks.
- **Personalised links** — generate per-recipient links that carry their own tracking.
- **Analytics** — capture detailed engagement data to feed back into proposals and embeds.
- **Data layer** — backed by **Azure Cosmos DB** (NoSQL) for flexible, schema-light storage.

This was an earlier (v1) platform in the suite, and a lot of the lessons here — especially around personalisation and analytics — fed directly into the products that came after it.
