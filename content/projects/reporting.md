**Reporting** lets agents view and share interactive online reports with prospective tenants for their industrial and office listings, or present a polished monthly listing report.

## What I worked on

- **Interactive web reports** — a shareable, link-based report experience built in Next.js and TypeScript.
- **PDF generation** — pixel-considered exports using **React PDF**, so the same report works both on screen and on paper.
- **Data-driven** — reports pull straight from the shared real-estate data, so numbers are always current.

The interesting tension here was making one report system that's genuinely good both as an interactive web page and as a printable PDF — two very different rendering models that had to stay visually consistent.
